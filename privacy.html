Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskNova collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our platform.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use TaskNova, we may collect:

  • Your name, username and email address.
  • Your country and account registration details.
  • Referral codes and referral activity.
  • Task submissions and proof you provide.
  • Wallet balances, transactions and withdrawal details.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and basic usage data.

2. How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide available tasks and record submissions.
  • Review task completion and calculate eligible rewards.
  • Process and review withdrawal requests.
  • Manage referrals and prevent abuse.
  • Maintain platform security and improve our services.
  • Communicate important account and service information.

3. Account and financial information

TaskNova records wallet activity, approved rewards and withdrawal requests to operate the platform. Payment details you submit are used to review and process your withdrawal requests.

Do not submit passwords, PINs or other sensitive payment credentials in task proof or public areas of the platform.

4. Cookies and technical information

TaskNova and its service providers may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, support security and operate the website.

5. Service providers

TaskNova uses third-party services, including Supabase, for authentication, database storage and platform operations. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to deliver the service and subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers needed to operate TaskNova, or where disclosure is required by law, necessary to protect users, or needed to investigate suspected fraud or security incidents.

7. Data security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect account information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your password private and use a strong, unique password.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate your account, maintain financial and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

You may contact TaskNova through the official support channel provided on the platform to make a privacy request.

10. Children's privacy

TaskNova is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use online services in their jurisdiction. Do not create an account if you are not legally eligible to use the platform.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the TaskNova support team through the official support channel provided on the platform.
