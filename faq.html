Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TaskNova, completing tasks, earning rewards and withdrawals.

Getting Started

TaskNova is an online microtask platform where registered members can complete available tasks, submit proof and earn rewards after their submissions are approved.
Yes. Creating a TaskNova account is free. TaskNova does not require members to deposit money or purchase a subscription to register.
TaskNova is designed for a worldwide audience. However, the availability of individual tasks may depend on your country or the requirements set by the task creator.
Open the registration page, enter your details, accept the terms and conditions, and submit the form. If email confirmation is requested, confirm your email before signing in.

Tasks and Earnings

Available task categories may include social media tasks, video watching, website visits, surveys, product reviews, website testing, app installs and other quick online tasks.
Choose an available task, follow its instructions, complete the required activity and submit the requested proof. Your reward is added to your wallet only after the submission is approved.
Rewards are credited after an administrator reviews and approves your task submission. Submitting proof does not automatically guarantee approval.
A submission may be rejected if the instructions were not followed, the proof is incomplete, the activity cannot be verified or the submission does not meet the task requirements.

Withdrawals and Referrals

The minimum withdrawal amount on TaskNova is $5. You must have at least this amount in your available balance before requesting a withdrawal.
Open the Withdraw page, enter an amount of at least $5, provide your payment details and submit your request. Withdrawal requests are reviewed by the TaskNova administration team.
Withdrawal requests are processed manually. Processing time may vary depending on the review process and payment arrangements.
Share your referral link with others. If a referred member joins and meets the qualifying requirements set by TaskNova, you may receive a referral reward according to the current programme rules.
