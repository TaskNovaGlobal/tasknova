Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I earn on TaskNova?
Browse available tasks, follow the instructions and submit the required proof. Your reward is credited after an administrator reviews and approves your submission.
2. When will my task reward appear?
Rewards are added to your wallet after your task submission has been approved. Pending submissions do not count as available wallet balance.
3. What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum withdrawal amount on TaskNova is $5. You must have at least this amount in your available balance before requesting a withdrawal.
4. How long do withdrawals take?
Withdrawal requests are reviewed by the TaskNova administration team. Processing time may vary depending on the payment method and review process.
5. Why was my task rejected?
A submission may be rejected if the instructions were not followed, the proof is incomplete, or the submitted work cannot be verified.
6. How does the referral programme work?
Share your referral link with others. Referral rewards depend on the programme rules and qualifying activities configured by TaskNova.
7. Can I create multiple accounts?
Use only accounts permitted by TaskNova's terms. Suspicious or abusive account activity may lead to account restrictions.
8. I cannot log in. What should I do?
Check that your email and password are correct. If you forgot your password, use the password reset option on the login page.

Still Need Help?

If your issue is not covered above, contact the support team with your registered email address and a clear description of the problem.
